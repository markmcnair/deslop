# Banned Phrases

Every phrase below must be removed or replaced. Organized by category.

---

## Throat-clearing openers
Remove entirely. Start with your actual point.

- Here's the thing
- Here's the thing:
- Here's what [X]
- The uncomfortable truth is
- It turns out
- It turns out that
- Let me be clear
- The truth is
- I'll say it again
- I'm going to be honest
- Can we talk about
- Here's what I find interesting
- Here's the problem though
- In today's [X]
- In today's rapidly evolving
- In a world where
- In the ever-changing landscape
- At its core
- When it comes to
- It is worth noting that
- It is important to note that
- At the end of the day
- The reality is
- If we're being honest
- Let's be real
- Real talk
- Look, [as sentence opener]
- The honest answer is
- Let's cut through the noise
- Here's what nobody talks about
- Spoiler alert
- The short version is
- I'll be straight with you

## Emphasis crutches
Remove entirely. If the point matters, the content proves it.

- Full stop.
- Period.
- Let that sink in.
- This matters because
- Make no mistake
- Here's why that matters
- Read that again.
- I can't stress this enough
- This is huge.
- This changes everything.

## Performative candor
Cut the candor announcement. Just be direct.

- creeps in
- I promise
- They exist I promise
- This is genuinely hard
- This is what leadership actually looks like
- actually matters
- if I'm being honest
- to be perfectly candid
- the part nobody mentions

## Sycophantic artifacts
Remove. These are chatbot conversation remnants.

- Great question!
- Excellent point!
- That's a really important question
- You're absolutely right
- I hope this helps!
- Let me know if you'd like me to expand
- Would you like me to elaborate?
- Of course!
- Certainly!
- Absolutely!
- Here is a [X] about [topic]

## Knowledge-cutoff disclaimers
Remove. State what you know or don't.

- As of [date]
- Up to my last training update
- While specific details are limited
- While specific details are scarce
- Based on available information
- According to publicly available sources
- To the best of my knowledge

- Let me walk you through
- In this section we'll

- Let's take a closer look
- Let's break this down

## Hedging stacks
Reduce to one qualifier maximum.

- could potentially possibly
- might arguably perhaps
- it could be argued that
- one might reasonably suggest
- it is perhaps worth considering
- there is reason to believe that
- it would not be unreasonable to say

## Email and message tells
Remove or rewrite. These show up in emails, Slack, and texts written by an assistant. People rarely write them to someone they know.

**Openers**
- I hope this finds you well
- I hope this email finds you well
- I hope you're doing well (as a reflex opener, not a real question)
- Just wanted to reach out
- Just wanted to follow up
- Just wanted to circle back
- Just wanted to touch base
- I wanted to take a moment to
- Thank you for reaching out
- Thanks so much for your email
- Per my last email
- As per our conversation

**Closers**
- Happy to help with anything
- Happy to help with anything else you need
- Don't hesitate to reach out
- Please don't hesitate to let me know
- Feel free to reach out if you have any questions
- Let me know if you have any questions or concerns
- Looking forward to hearing from you
- Thanks again for your time and consideration
- Hope this helps!
- Let me know your thoughts!

**Over-thanking**
- More than one "thank you" in a short message
- "Thank you so much for your patience and understanding"
- "I really appreciate you taking the time to"
- "Thanks in advance for your help" stacked with another thanks

**Filler in messages**
- I just wanted to let you know that
- Quick question for you:
- To give you a bit of context,
- Hope you had a great weekend! (when it leads straight into business)
- Circling back on this
- Bumping this to the top of your inbox
